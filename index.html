How to Play Suika Watermelon Game

Game Rules

The rules of Suika Watermelon game are very simple! You have to make a bigger fruit by combining two same small fruits such as Apple.

When two fruits of the same type touch each other, they will merge into a larger fruit. The goal is to create the largest fruit possible - the watermelon!

Scoring System

Each fruit has a different point value. The larger the fruit, the more points you earn when you create it.

Try to create chain reactions by combining multiple fruits at once to maximize your score!
